  Both are internally consistent; documenting it avoids a false alarm later.
* **FRED changed its SP500 download to the last 12 months** (licensing). load_fred now keeps a local cache
  and backfills older history from Yahoo's ^GSPC (identical to 1e-7). Without that fix the reports broke.

  cannot act before the 30 Nov close; the daily 200-day account exited on 19 Nov and skipped Friday's -11%.
  One week is noise for growth, but it is exactly the risk the backtests show (1987, 2020).
